Recognize the warning signs

Busy business. Unreliable numbers.

01

Expenses and income counted twice

Bank feeds, imported files and manual entries overlap. A paid bill still appears overdue, or a deposit becomes a second sale.

We trace the original entry, payment and bank activity before recommending the correction.

02

Bookkeeping you cannot rely on

Uncategorized expenses, stale balances, inconsistent vendor names and months of unreconciled activity make every report questionable.

We work through the agreed period and document what is corrected and what still needs evidence.

03

Jobs look profitable until cash runs out

Materials, labor, equipment and subcontractors are mixed together. Costs disappear into overhead, and billing does not tell the whole story.

We agree job and cost categories, trace available records and separate bookkeeping cleanup from optional profitability analysis.

When a file is described as corrupt

First establish what is wrong.

Duplicate transactions and incorrect postings are accounting data problems. A company file that will not open, fails integrity checks or contains damaged records needs a technical assessment first.

A separate Desktop recovery track

For QuickBooks Desktop, we preserve the original file, obtain a backup and assess Intuit’s Verify/Rebuild route. We verify again after repair and compare key reports. Unresolved damage is referred to Intuit or a specialist recovery provider under an agreed scope.

The cleanup tool is not a binary company-file repair utility. Recovery feasibility and any third-party cost are assessed before a repair commitment. Assisted Payroll cases are referred to Intuit before rebuilding.

An example of the review

Similar entries do not always mean duplicates.

ILLUSTRATIVE FINDING

Two $2,400 material entries

A supplier bill and a bank-feed expense appear to represent the same purchase. Equal amounts alone are insufficient evidence.

OUR REVIEW AND DELIVERABLE

Trace the supplier invoice, bank debit and bill-payment links. If duplication is confirmed, correct the extra posting with approval and recheck accounts payable, the bank reconciliation and the affected job costs.

ILLUSTRATIVE FINDING

Material costs buried in office expenses

A receipt identifies a project, but the posting sits in a general overhead account with no job reference.

OUR REVIEW AND DELIVERABLE

Confirm the purchase and project with your records, propose the category and job assignment, and update supported fields. Deliver a correction log and a revised job-cost schedule where included in scope.

ILLUSTRATIVE FINDING

The customer paid, but the invoice remains open

A receipt is recorded without the correct invoice linkage. A bank deposit may also have been entered as income.

OUR REVIEW AND DELIVERABLE

Check remittance details, invoice balances and bank deposits. Apply the supported payment, investigate any duplicate income and review the revised receivables aging.

Clear expectations

Questions before we begin.

Will the tool change everything automatically?

No. We validate the connection, review candidates against records and obtain authorization for the agreed corrections. Some reviewed workspaces contain demo data. A feature visible in the application is not evidence that it has been validated against your live books.

Can you guarantee every corrupt file can be recovered?

No. File recovery depends on the damage, backups, edition and specialist support. We first diagnose the issue and agree the recovery route. Accounting cleanup follows once the file is usable.

Will you change payroll, taxes or closed years?

Only under an explicitly agreed scope and with the necessary customer and accountant approval. Tax filings, amended returns, legal determinations and statutory audit opinions are outside the standard cleanup service.

What if receipts or statements are missing?

We identify the gaps and request replacements or confirmation. Unsupported amounts remain on an exception register; we do not invent balancing entries simply to make reports agree.

Can the same service support other industries?

Yes. The core method applies to retail, hospitality, professional services and other QuickBooks users. We adapt the account structure and controls to the business and country version. Construction job-cost analysis is an optional workstream.
